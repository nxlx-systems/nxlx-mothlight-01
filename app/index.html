Mothlight No.01

Light safety. Mothlight No.01 makes moving and changing light patterns. If you or anyone watching has photosensitive epilepsy, or a pattern makes you feel unwell, stop and look away. Hold Blackout to stop the picture.

How to play it

  • The tabs run left to right in the order the signal flows: Source, Osc (the pattern), Trails, Move, Colour, LFO and Output.
  • Tap a fader twice to put it back. Tap a pad twice to centre it.
  • Presets P1 to P8: hold to save, tap to recall.
  • Blackout: hold to go black, tap to come back.
  • Perform shows the picture alone. Tap the picture, then Show controls, to come back.
  • Random only changes the groups lit on the Source page.
  • MIDI learn: tap MIDI learn, tap a control, then move a knob. It needs a browser with Web MIDI, such as Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android. Safari does not have it.
  • With a keyboard: arrow keys move a fader or pad, Delete puts it back, Enter recalls a preset and Shift and Enter saves one.

Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone or iPad: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android phones and tablets, Fire included: open the browser menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computers: in Chrome or Edge, use the install button in the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock. In other browsers, bookmark it.

Once installed it opens with no internet. Your settings and presets stay in this browser on this device. Tried so far on an Amazon Fire 7 (2019) and an iPhone 17 Pro; other devices are not yet tested.
